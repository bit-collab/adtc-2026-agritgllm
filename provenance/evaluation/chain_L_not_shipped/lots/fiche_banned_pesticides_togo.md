# banned_pesticides_togo

## category
input_regulation

## crop_or_species
all crops

## regions
whole country

## season
all year

## symptoms
A merchant, a neighbour or a label offers Atrazine, Paraquat or Glyphosate, or a drum with no label said to be one of them.

## cause
Atrazine and Paraquat are banned in Togo: since 2018 their importation and distribution are forbidden on the national territory, and Glyphosate has also been banned. In 2019 more than 13 tonnes of Atrazine were seized.

## spread
The products still circulate through informal sellers. The Ministry of Agriculture asks farmers to report any sale of these products.

## management
1. Do not buy, use or keep Atrazine, Paraquat or Glyphosate; they are banned in Togo.
2. Report the seller to the agricultural services (ICAT) or the local authorities.
3. For weed control, use only a product on the official list of approved (homologated) products kept by the national pesticide committee; ICAT can say which product is approved and give its rate.
4. A drum with no label is never approved: take it to ICAT rather than use it.

## prevention
1. Buy inputs only from registered dealers who show the approved-products list.
2. Read the label; an approved product has a name, an active substance, a registration number and a rate.

## confusable_with
An approved herbicide with a different active substance, sold under a trade name; only the official list tells them apart.

## when_to_refer
ICAT for the approved product, its rate, and for reporting a seller of a banned product.
